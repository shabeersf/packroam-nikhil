Field Itinerary

Meghalaya Uncharted Expeditions

Duration 6D / 5N
Stay Tribal Homestays
Difficulty Moderate
Starting Price ₹24,999
Day 01

Arrival, Hill Drives & Khasi Welcome Feast

Pick up from transit hub in private expedition 4x4. Scenic ascent through misty gorges with freshly brewed spiced local tea and authentic dinner at our partner homestay.

Day 02

Secret Ridge Trail, Natural Aqueducts & Waterfalls

Descend ancient stone stairways away from standard day-tourists. Swim in hidden plunge pools and learn ancestral forest navigation from our village elder guide.

Day 03

Bio-Living Root Architecture & Stargazing Camp

Marvel at two-century-old living Ficus root bridges woven across roaring gorges. Evening acoustic storytelling around campfire under unfiltered dark skies.

Day 04

Crystal River Boating, Limestone Caves & Farewell Hearth

Early morning paddle across glass-clear river waters. Explore subterranean limestone formations, followed by community farewell dinner and souvenir field logs.
